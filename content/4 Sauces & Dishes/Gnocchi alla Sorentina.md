SERVES 4 to 6

Okay, not a lasagne, but similar in spirit and too good to pass up.

## Ingredients

- [ ] 1 batch Potato Gnocchi
- [ ] 1 batch All-Purpose Tomato Sauce
- [ ] 1 pound (450 grams) fresh mozzarella, cut into cubes
- [ ] Fresh basil
- [ ] 1 ounce (30 grams) finely grated Pecorino Romano (optional)

## Directions

1. Boil the gnocchi in a large pot of well-salted water until they float. 
2. Use a spider sieve or slotted spoon to transfer the gnocchi to the tomato sauce and gently stir to coat. 
3. Scoop half of the gnocchi into a 9 x 13-inch baking dish and scatter half of the mozzarella and a few torn basil leaves on top. Then layer on the remaining gnocchi and mozzarella, and sprinkle with Pecorino. 
4. Bake at 400°F/205°C until the cheese melts, 10 to 15 minutes. Broil briefly until the top is golden. Serve with more basil.
