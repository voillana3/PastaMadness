# [[Pasta alla Genovese]]

### Ingredients

- 1 1/2 pounds boneless beef chuck roast, trimmed and cut into 4-by-3-inch pieces
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
- 2 small carrots, small dice
- 1 celery rib, small dice
- 1 lbs red onions, thinly sliced\*
- 3 lbs yellow onions, thinly sliced\*
- 1 Tbsp tomato paste
- 2 cup dry white wine
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 pound short tubular dried pasta such as ziti, paccheri, or rigatoni
- Finely grated Pecorino Romano or Parmigiano-Reggiano

\*I prefer a combination of red and yellow onions.  You can also make this recipe with entirely yellow or white onions.

### Directions

1. Season beef all over with salt and pepper; set aside and let sit for at least 45 minutes before cooking.  Slice the onions and prepare the other ingredients while you wait.
2. In a large Dutch oven, preheat on high and once hot, add the olive oil.  Then brown all sides of the beef really well.  Sear in batches, if needed.  Once seared, remove the beef from the pot.
3. Adjust the heat to medium-high and add carrots, celery, and a small handful of onions.  Cook, stirring occasionally, until they begin to brown.
4. Add tomato paste and stir it in to coat the vegetables.
5. Add beef, stir everything together.
6. Add in white wine and cook until it reduces by about half.
7. Add bay leaves and remaining onions.
8. Season with salt and stir.
9. Cook uncovered for a few minutes so the onions can steam and begin to cook down.  Then add the lid to cook for a few minutes. Stir.
10. Adjust heat to low if using the stove.  Preheat oven to 300 F if using the oven.
11. Cook, covered, for 2 hours but stir once every 30 minutes.
12. Cook uncovered for 2 hours, but stir once every 30 minutes.  If the sauce is drying out, add some wine or water. The beef should be fork tender.  Remove from the pot to shred and then stir it back into the pot.
13. Taste for seasoning.
14. In a pot of salted, boiling water, cook the short dried pasta to about 1-2 shorted than al dente.
15. In another pan or saucier, we will marry the sauce and pasta.  Add in about a cup of the sauce and the pasta.  Add pasta water as needed to adjust the consistency.  You can also add a knob of butter or basil. Finish cooking the pasta in the sauce.
16. Remove from the heat and let the pasta cool for a short moment before adding in the cheese.  Stir vigorously to meld the cheese into the sauce.
