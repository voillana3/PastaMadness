# [[Double Cream Sauce]]

_For stuffed pastas, such as [[Cappelletti]], [[Classic Ravioli]], and [[Cannelloni]]._

### Ingredients

- 7 Tbsp cream
- 2 Tbsp butter
- Salt

**[[Cream Sauce]]**

- 2 Tbsp butter
- 1/4 Cup flour
- 1 Cup chicken broth

### Directions

Make a [[Cream Sauce]] using half the butter, the flour, and chicken broth listed above.

For the last 10 minutes of cooking, add half the cream a little at a time, always stirring. When the sauce has thickened well, remove it from the heat and taste it. Add salt if you need it. Pour in the remaining cream and the rest of the butter in small pieces, always stirring.
