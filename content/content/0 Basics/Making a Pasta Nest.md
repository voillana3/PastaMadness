Gather the ends of about 15 strands in the palm of your non-dominant hand. Using the other hand, loosely wrap the strands around your non-dominant hand as many times as you can. Position your hand with the pasta on your work surface or sheet pan and let go—the ribbons should release into a small nest. For shorter strands like tagliolini, simply fold them in half wallet-style into a U shape.
