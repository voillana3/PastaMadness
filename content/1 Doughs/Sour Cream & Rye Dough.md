**Shapes:**
60g (1/4 cup) full-fat sour cream
70 mL (1/4 cup plus 1 tablespoon) tepid water
2 large eggs
150g rye flour
250g 00 or all-purpose flour

Whisk the sour cream, water, and eggs together in a bowl until well combined.  Then make the dough using this mixture as the liquid.
