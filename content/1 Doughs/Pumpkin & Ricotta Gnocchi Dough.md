225 grams (8 ounces) high-quality full-fat ricotta (or make your own, here)
225 grams (8 ounces) pumpkin purée
55 grams (2 ounces) finely grated Parmigiano-Reggiano or Grana Padano
1 egg plus 1 yolk, lightly beaten
3 grams (1 teaspoon) kosher salt
Whole nutmeg
150 grams all-purpose flour

Thoroughly pat the ricotta dry with paper towels, and do the same with the pumpkin purée (it’s essential to sop up the pumpkin’s moisture; use a few changes of paper towels!). Make the dough as directed for the Ricotta Gnocchi Dough
